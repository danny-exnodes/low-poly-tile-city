## 1. Reddit r/proceduralgeneration (cho phép khoe dự án; KHÔNG bán thẳng trong bài)
**Title:** I wrote a tiny software 3D renderer in Flutter to draw a cosy procedural town (runs in the browser)

**Body:**
No 3D engine: meshes are low-poly boxes, each triangle is lit by a sun + sky model with seasons and night lights, then painter's-algorithm sorted and flushed in a few `Canvas.drawVertices` calls. Town layout, buildings, cars on lanes, pedestrians, wind with flying leaves and a "leaf devil" are all procedural from a seed. You can build tiles, swap in world landmarks and share your town as a ~170-char link.

Demo: https://danny-exnodes.github.io/low-poly-tile-city/
Happy to answer questions about the renderer. (We are also open to licensing it / custom builds if anyone needs one.)
